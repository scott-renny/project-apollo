# Backup and Recovery

## Objectives

- Recover essential personal and project data after loss, damage, or replacement.
- Avoid making either Apollo device the only copy of critical recovery material.
- Confirm that backups are encrypted, current, and restorable.

## Back up

- approved contacts, calendars, tasks, notes, and selected media;
- application data only where the vendor provides secure supported backup;
- sanitized branding and portfolio assets;
- device configuration notes without secrets.

Do not place recovery codes, authenticator seeds, private keys, decrypted vault exports, or unprotected VPN profiles in general-purpose cloud backup.

## Recovery preparation

- Maintain an offline inventory of critical accounts and recovery methods.
- Ensure remote-device services can be accessed without the lost device.
- Keep at least one independent phishing-resistant authentication method.
- Document carrier, vendor, and account-support routes privately.
- Verify that encrypted backups can be restored to a test target or replacement workflow.

## Test cadence

- Monthly: verify backup completion and storage capacity.
- Quarterly: restore a non-sensitive sample and confirm file integrity.
- Annually or after major change: rehearse replacement-device recovery.

## Recovery order

1. Secure identities and carrier account.
2. Enroll and update the replacement device.
3. Configure screen lock, account MFA, remote finding, and secure access.
4. Restore essential data from a verified backup.
5. Reinstall only approved applications.
6. Reissue device-specific keys, certificates, and sessions.
7. Validate NetWatch and alerting with least privilege.
8. Review old-device sessions and revoke anything no longer required.

Record results privately; publish only sanitized lessons learned.
