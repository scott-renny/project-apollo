# Synchronization Policy

## Approved for synchronization

- calendar and approved contacts;
- tasks, reminders, bookmarks, and project notes;
- password-manager vault through its supported encrypted sync;
- selected photos, screenshots, and documents;
- non-secret branding assets and sanitized portfolio files.

## Keep separate or tightly controlled

- device home-screen layouts and mode settings;
- recovery codes and break-glass credentials;
- authenticator exports and high-risk backup material;
- SSH private keys and VPN profiles unless the selected system explicitly secures them;
- tablet-only administrative tools;
- unnecessary personal media and downloads;
- raw screenshots and diagnostics.

## Rules

- Use supported encrypted synchronization, not ad hoc messaging or public links.
- Minimize offline copies.
- Require screen lock and account MFA before enabling sync.
- Review connected devices and sessions quarterly.
- Test deletion propagation and recovery behavior with non-sensitive sample data.
